Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At RexOnSolar ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or inquire about our solar energy services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical installation address when you request a solar quote or consultation.
  • Property & Energy Details: Monthly electricity bill estimates, roof type, and property ownership status to help us design an accurate solar system.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited through cookies or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide customized solar energy estimates and site survey schedules.
  • Assist with government solar subsidies, permits, and utility net-metering applications.
  • Respond to customer service inquiries and provide post-installation support.
  • Improve our website functionality and customer experience.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your details only with trusted partners necessary to complete your solar installation, such as:

  • Certified installation engineers and site surveyors.
  • Electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) and government portals for net-metering and subsidy processing.
  • Authorized financing partners (only if you explicitly request solar loan assistance).

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us. To exercise these rights or opt out of promotional communications, please contact us using the details below.
